# Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Liturgical Guide (ሥርዓተ ቅዳሴና ጸሎት)

## 1. Kidase (ቅዳሴ - Divine Liturgy)
The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church uses 14 authorized Anaphoras (አኰቴተ ቍርባን / ቅዳሴያት):
1. **ቅዳሴ ሐዋርያት (Anaphora of the Apostles)** - The standard and most frequently celebrated liturgy.
2. **ቅዳሴ እግዚእ (Anaphora of the Lord)** - Celebrated on major feasts of Our Lord.
3. **ቅዳሴ ማርያም (Anaphora of Our Lady Mary by Abba Heryacos)** - Celebrated on feasts of Saint Mary.
4. **ቅዳሴ ዮሐንስ ወልደ ነጐድጓድ (Anaphora of St. John the Evangelist)**
5. **ቅዳሴ ሠለስቱ ምዕት (Anaphora of the 318 Fathers of Nicaea)**
6. **ቅዳሴ አትናቴዎስ (Anaphora of St. Athanasius)**
7. **ቅዳሴ ባስልዮስ (Anaphora of St. Basil the Great)**
8. **ቅዳሴ ጐርጎርዮስ (Anaphora of St. Gregory the Armenian)**
9. **ቅዳሴ ኤጲፋንዮስ (Anaphora of St. Epiphanius)**
10. **ቅዳሴ ዮሐንስ አፈወርቅ (Anaphora of St. John Chrysostom)**
11. **ቅዳሴ ቄርሎስ (Anaphora of St. Cyril of Alexandria)**
12. **ቅዳሴ ያዕቆብ ዘሥሩግ (Anaphora of St. Jacob of Serug)**
13. **ቅዳሴ ዲዮስቆሮስ (Anaphora of St. Dioscorus of Alexandria)**
14. **ቅዳሴ ጐርጎርዮስ ካልዕ (Anaphora of St. Gregory of Nazianzus)**

### Structure of the Liturgy
- **ሥርዓተ ነዋየ ቅድሳት (Preparation / Prothesis)**
- **ትምህርተ ኅቡአትና ጸሎት (Prayers & Hymns)**
- **ግብረ ሐዋርያትና ወንጌል (Epistles and Holy Gospel)**
- **አኰቴተ ቍርባን (Anaphora proper & Eucharistic consecration)**
- **ፍሬ ቅዳሴና ሥርዓተ ቍርባን (Fraction & Holy Communion)**
- **ጸሎተ አኮቴትና ቡራኬ (Thanksgiving & Final Blessing)**

## 2. Wudase Mariam (ውዳሴ ማርያም - Praises of Saint Mary)
Organized into 7 daily sections composed by Saint Ephrem the Syrian (ቅዱስ ኤፍሬም ሶርያዊ), supplemented by St. Yared:
- **ሰኑይ (Monday)**: God's counsel of salvation and creation of Adam.
- **ሠሉስ (Tuesday)**: The Burning Bush and the Ark of the Covenant.
- **ረቡዕ (Wednesday)**: The Fleece of Gideon and the pure vessel.
- **ሐሙስ (Thursday)**: The Rod of Aaron that budded without planting.
- **ዓርብ (Friday)**: The Golden Censer and the Mercy Seat.
- **ቀዳሚት (Saturday)**: The City of the Great King and Mount Zion.
- **እሑድ (Sunday)**: The Gate of Ezekiel facing East, opened for Christ alone.

Plus:
- **አንቀጸ ብርሃን (The Gate of Light by St. Yared)**
- **ይዌድስዋ መላእክት (The Angels Praise Her)**

## 3. The 7 Canonical Fasts (ሰባቱ አጽዋማት)
1. **ዐቢይ ጾም (Great Lent / Hudadi)** - 55 days.
2. **ጾመ ሐዋርያት (Fast of the Apostles)**.
3. **ጾመ ፍልሰታ (Fast of the Assumption of St. Mary)** - 16 days (Nahase 1-16).
4. **ጾመ ነቢያት (Fast of the Prophets / Advent)** - 44 days (Hidar 15 - Tahsas 28).
5. **ጾመ ነነዌ (Fast of Nineveh)** - 3 days.
6. **ጾመ ጋድ (Gehad / Eve of Epiphany and Christmas)**.
7. **ጾመ ድኅነት (Fast of Salvation)** - Wednesdays and Fridays throughout the year (except Pentecost season).
